# Free resources, in the order to use them

Everything below is free on prepnplaced.com, listed in the order to use it.

## 1. The free book

[Data Engineering: From Zero to Production & Interview Ready](https://www.prepnplaced.com/data-engineering-book?utm_source=github&utm_medium=oss_repo&utm_campaign=de_roadmap_2026) by Durgesh Yadav. A 244-page PDF that follows one order through a fictional Indian e-commerce company and brings in each tool when the story needs it: the terminal, SQL, Python, data modelling, storage formats, the cloud, pipelines, Spark, Airflow, Kafka, data quality, a portfolio project and the interview loop. Every figure in it was measured, and the scripts behind them are in the public [Field Kit](https://github.com/analyticsdurgesh/data-engineering-book-field-kit) repo. Downloading takes one Google sign-in.

New to all of this? Read Lecture Zero and Chapter 1, then Chapter 3 for SQL.

## 2. The roadmap pages

- [Data Engineering Roadmap 2026](https://www.prepnplaced.com/data-engineering-roadmap-2026): the month-by-month plan this repo condenses, with FAQs, certifications and salary bands.
- [Data Engineer Roadmap](https://www.prepnplaced.com/data-engineer-roadmap): the skill order, a 30/60/90-day plan and the projects that make resume proof.
- [How to Become a Data Engineer in India](https://www.prepnplaced.com/how-to-become-data-engineer-india): step by step, with fresher and career-switcher paths and campus versus off-campus.
- [2026 Tech Career Roadmaps](https://www.prepnplaced.com/tech-career-roadmaps-2026): the data engineer roadmap as a PDF, and a Top 50 interview questions deck. No login.

- [Free PDF notes](https://www.prepnplaced.com/prepnplaced-notes): Spark, PySpark, SQL, Kafka, Snowflake, Airflow, dbt, Azure, AWS, warehousing and system design. Browsing is free; downloading takes one Google sign-in. Use them for revision in the week before an interview.

## Official documentation

When something breaks, read the vendor's own documentation. Spark, Airflow, Kafka, dbt, Snowflake and Databricks all publish theirs for free, and interviewers can tell who has read it.

## When paying makes sense

The free path covers the syllabus. People pay for a deadline, a small batch, and someone who reads their code and says what is wrong with it. If you have finished a self-paced course before without stalling, you do not need to pay. PrepNPlaced's [Data Engineering Resources](https://www.prepnplaced.com/data-engineering-resources) page links a comparison of paid options sold in India, its own cohort included.
